Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go! You can request deletion of your Zantray account and all associated data by contacting us.

What gets deleted:

  • ✅ Your profile (name, email, photo, bio)
  • ✅ All your messages
  • ✅ All your status posts
  • ✅ All your Saj AI conversations
  • ✅ Your location data
  • ✅ All account settings and preferences

⚠️ This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account:

Option 1 — Email us: Send an email to zantray.support@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Account" and include your registered email address. We will delete your account within 30 days.

Option 2 — In the app: Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account (coming soon in v1.1)

📧 Request Deletion 🌴 Keep My Account

After submitting your request, we will confirm via email and delete your data within 30 days. Some data may be retained for legal compliance purposes.
