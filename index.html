How to use Saj AI effectively

Get the most out of your AI assistant — tips and tricks

TIP 01
Ask in your language

You can type in Haitian Creole, English, French or Spanish. Saj AI understands all 4 perfectly.

"Kijan pou m voye lajan ann Ayiti?"
TIP 02
Scan any document

Tap the 📄 button and take a clear photo of your document in good lighting for best results.

Try: Tax letters, medical bills, legal notices
TIP 03
Be specific

The more detail you give Saj AI the better the answer. Include your situation for personalized help.

"I have chest pain when I exercise. What could this be?"
TIP 04
Check the sources

Saj AI always provides trusted source links. Click them to verify important information yourself.

📚 cdc.gov · uscis.gov · britannica.com
TIP 05
Save your history

All Saj AI conversations are saved. Tap 📜 History to find important information from past chats.

Great for revisiting medical or legal advice
TIP 06
Switch languages

Tap the flag icon in Saj AI to switch the response language anytime during your conversation.
