Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: May 2026

🌴 Zantray is a messaging app built for the Haitian community. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we use your data.

📱 SMS & OTP Opt-In Policy

Zantray uses SMS one-time passwords (OTP) solely for account verification purposes. Here is how our opt-in process works:

How users opt in: Users voluntarily enter their phone number on the Zantray login screen and tap the "Send Verification Code" button. This action constitutes explicit opt-in consent to receive a one-time SMS verification code.

What we send: We only send a single 6-digit verification code via SMS. This is used exclusively to verify the user's identity during login. We do not send marketing messages, promotional content, or any other unsolicited SMS.

Frequency: SMS messages are only sent when a user explicitly requests a verification code by tapping the button. Users are never automatically subscribed to any messaging program.

How to opt out: Users can stop receiving SMS messages at any time by simply not requesting a verification code. No further action is required.

📋 By entering your phone number and tapping "Send Verification Code" on the Zantray app, you agree to receive a one-time SMS verification code for account authentication purposes only.

🔒 Data We Collect

We collect the following information to provide the Zantray service:

Phone number: Used for account creation and login verification via OTP SMS.

Name and profile photo: Displayed to your contacts within the app. You choose what to share.

Messages: Stored securely to enable real-time messaging between users.

Status updates: Shared with other Zantray users and automatically deleted after 24 hours.

Preferred language: Stored to personalize your Saj AI experience.

🧠 Saj AI Data

Conversations with Saj AI are stored to allow you to access your history. We do not share your Saj AI conversations with third parties. Your conversations are private and only accessible by you.

🔐 How We Protect Your Data

All data is stored securely using MongoDB Atlas with industry-standard encryption. We use JWT tokens for authentication and never store plain-text passwords. Your phone number is never shared with third parties.

📤 Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except as required by law. We use Twilio to deliver SMS verification codes — your phone number is shared with Twilio solely for this purpose.

🗑️ Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at zantray.support@gmail.com. We will process your request within 30 days.

👶 Children's Privacy

Zantray is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

📝 Terms of Service

By using Zantray you agree to use the app for lawful purposes only. You may not use Zantray to send spam, harass other users, or violate any applicable laws. We reserve the right to suspend accounts that violate these terms.
